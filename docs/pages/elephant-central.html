Homes with the view

Located in Zone 1, almost all of our homes at Elephant Central benefit from views of iconic London landmarks – old and new. Rain or shine, whether you're waking up to the Shard or to Big Ben, it's hard not to have your breath taken away.

Across all three buildings at Elephant Central: Mawes House, Raglan House and Tantallon House, we offer a choice of furnished 1-3 bed apartments to rent, that are already set-up with Wi-Fi and energy efficient features* helping you reduce your consumption, keeping bills and emissions low.

*99% of Elephant Central apartments are rated B on EPC

Getting around South London

Despite being renowned for its rather puzzling roundabout, Elephant and Castle is a key transport hub in South London:

  • 20 minute walk to South Bank and London Bridge
  • Access Northern and Bakerloo lines in less than 5 minutes from your door
  • 28 bus routes spanning the capital run through Elephant and Castle
  • National Rail train services from across the road
  • It's on the Cycle Superhighway, connecting to King's Cross
