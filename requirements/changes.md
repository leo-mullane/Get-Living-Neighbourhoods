# Elephant Central: proposed changes

Source of truth for the copy is `src/js/tweaks.js`. This list is for reference and traceability.

- **T01 Give the hero a next step** (`hero-cta`, round 1, Check with developers): Add a See available homes button and an Enquire now button to the hero, and lift the white wash off the photograph.
- **T02 Put price and availability in the first screen** (`hero-facts`, round 1, Check with developers): A short line of facts under the subheading: one and two bed homes from £2,550 pcm, eight homes available, furnished, Wi-Fi included.
- **T03 Move the homes up the page** (`homes-up`, round 1, Check with developers): Move Apartments to rent at Elephant Central from two-thirds of the way down the page to directly below the hero.
- **T04 Prices and availability on the apartment cards** (`card-prices`, round 1, Check with developers): Each card shows a from-price and how many homes are available, and its button says what it does. Where nothing is available, as with three-bed homes today, the card offers to register interest instead.
- **T05 Lead with the amenities renters weigh most** (`amenities`, round 3, Check with developers): Show eight amenities, led by the ones renters weigh most, with a link to show all 14.
- **T06 Show the enquiry form instead of hiding it** (`enquiry-open`, round 2, Check with developers): The enquiry block follows the homes and views rather than the community carousel, the form is open on arrival, and a line above it says what happens after you send it.
- **T07 Ask for less before the first conversation** (`form-fields`, round 2, HubSpot setting): Keep the fields the lettings team needs in order to reply, and make the rest optional: preferred contact method, pets, budget, employment status, how they heard about Get Living, and whether they live in the UK. Required fields fall from 13 to 8.
- **T08 Put the residents' rating where people decide** (`proof`, round 2, CMS edit): A line of social proof beside the apartment cards and above the form: residents rate Get Living 4.42 out of 5 on HomeViews, from 1,357 verified reviews.
- **T09 Lead the transport section with journey times** (`transport`, round 3, CMS edit): Each line of the transport list starts with the time, the section gets a button to the commute calculator, and the calculator moves up to sit directly beneath it.
- **T10 Point the sticky bar at the homes** (`subnav`, round 1, Check with developers): The sticky Elephant Central bar gets a View homes button next to Enquire now, and its links follow the new page order with homes first.
- **T11 Two actions in the mobile bar, and move the chat bubble** (`mobile-bar`, round 2, Small front-end change): On phones the bottom bar swaps the Elephant Central logo for two buttons, View homes and Enquire, and the chat bubble becomes a small button inside the bar so it no longer sits over the content.
- **T12 A next step at the end of each section** (`section-ctas`, round 3, Check with developers): Sections that end without an action, community and Shop and Eat, finish with a See available homes link, and the page closes with a short enquiry prompt before the building works notice.
